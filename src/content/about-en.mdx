**Evdeki** is a journal about life, work, and the many living things that share an old family home.

Hello, I’m Göker. I’m a computer engineer with a PhD, along with academic backgrounds in graphic design, photography, and agriculture. I live and work in a house with a large garden that has been in my family since my great-grandfather’s time.

Working from home means that no two days are quite the same. A normal day might involve writing code, developing a side project, repairing part of the house, observing an unfamiliar insect in the garden, caring for the animals, or trying to understand why something has suddenly stopped working.

This publication is where I document those days.

You can expect posts about:

- Life in an old family house and the work required to keep it alive
- Gardening, plants, insects, and seasonal changes
- Cats, dogs, chickens, koi, and other visiting creatures
- DIY projects, repairs, restoration, and home decoration
- The pool, ponds, outdoor spaces, and small experiments around the property
- Software, self-hosting, creative coding, and independent side projects
- Photography, design, and the process of making things
- The moods, interruptions, discoveries, and occasional chaos of working from home

Some posts will be practical, some observational, and others simply records of moments worth remembering. Together, they form an evolving account of a home—not only as a building, but as a living environment shaped by people, animals, plants, technology, weather, and time.

Even the website reflects this idea: it is powered by an old MacBook Air that I turned into a home server and built using the open-source [Substack](https://github.com/uygullama/substack) template developed under **[uyguLama](https://uygulama.net/)**.

### Follow life around the house

New stories, projects, observations, and experiments will arrive directly in your inbox. You can also use the [Substack app](https://substack.com/app/app-store-redirect) for an ad-free reading experience, audio, discussions, and other community features.

### Support the garden, animals, and projects

Paid subscriptions directly support the things documented here: caring for the garden and animals, maintaining and improving the house, and funding independent creative and technical projects.

In other words, your support does not disappear into an abstract publication budget. It returns to the place, its inhabitants, and the experiments that make these stories possible.

If you enjoy old houses, gardens, animals, technology, DIY work, creative experiments, or simply the unpredictable texture of everyday life, you are welcome here.

Share your own experiences, offer advice, ask questions, or join the conversation in the comments.
